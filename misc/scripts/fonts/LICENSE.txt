The fonts in this directory are used only by game_master_assets.py to bake the
author credit into main/splash.png. Both are released under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org):

  Rye-Regular.ttf            - Rye (Sorkin Type)
  NotoSansBengali-Bold.ttf   - Noto Sans Bengali (Google/monotype)

Keep the files together with the script: the credit splash is regenerated from
them and cannot be rebuilt without them.
